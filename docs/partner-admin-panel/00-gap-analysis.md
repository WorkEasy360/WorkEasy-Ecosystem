# Partner Admin Panel (specification of 08 November 2026): audit and gap analysis

Branch `feat/partner-admin-panel`, cut from `feat/admin-panel` at `dccf643` (the live release `2026.11.10-dccf643`). Written 2026-11-11, before any change. Decisions: [ADR-063](../architecture/adrs/ADR-063-partner-admin-panel-eight-menus-wallet-and-onboarding.md). Results: [status.md](status.md).

The audit read the backend (partners, commissions, wallets, payments, subscriptions, provisioning, administration), the partner portal (`frontend/src/features/partnerpanel`) and the Admin's partner screens (`frontend/src/features/controlplane`). "Exists" below means a real screen on a real endpoint; nothing here is mock data.

## What already works and is kept

| Area | Where |
|---|---|
| New Partner form with **Partner Email**, name, type, country, mobile, invitation or manual password, draft on creation | `controlplane/addpartner/AddPartnerForm.tsx`, `controlplane/partner_onboarding.py` (ADR-061) |
| KYC with the six states Draft, Submitted, Under Review, Correction Required, Approved, Rejected; reviewer, decision date, decision log, document access log, fail-closed malware scan | `partners/kyc.py`, `PartnerKyc`, `PartnerKycDecision`, `PartnerKycDocumentAccess` (ADR-060/061) |
| Account states Draft, Active, Suspended, Terminated, separate from KYC; activation refused until KYC is approved | `contracts/enums.PartnerStatus`, `kyc.assert_may_activate` |
| Server-side partner permissions, resolved on every request from the partner's status, the member's role and the partner's grants; a role can never exceed the partner's grants | `partners/resolver.py`, `capabilities.permissions_for_role` |
| Login as Guest: a database record, 30 minutes, one-time ticket across hosts, read-only middleware, redactions, revocation, exit back to the Admin | `partners/guest.py`, `interfaces/api/guest_session.py` (ADR-062) |
| Shared append-only wallet ledger with row locks, idempotency keys, no-overdraft constraint, reversals that name the original | `wallets/ledger.py`, `wallets/models.py` (ADR-060) |
| Referral links and codes, campaigns, capture only for an active partner holding the grant, conversions, commission accrual and clearance, payouts with maker-checker, withdrawals with sealed bank/UPI details and staff verification | `commissions/*`, `partners/withdrawals.py`, `controlplane/partner_withdrawals.py` |
| Reseller customers: create workspace (idempotent), owner invitation (recognises an existing account), sell a plan, change plan, cancel at period end, partner orders | `partners/customers.py`, `PartnerScreens.tsx` |
| Support tickets to Workeasy360, a customer desk for sellers, notifications | `partners/tickets.py`, `Helpdesk.tsx` |
| Staff: invite, role, suspend, remove; My account: password, sessions | `Team`, `Account.tsx` |
| White-label branding, logo, domains with DNS verification | `PartnerScreens.tsx`, `partners/domains.py` |

## Gaps, by section of the specification

| § | Requirement | Found | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Partner Email label, fields | Live since ADR-061 | None. White Label is offered as a type that grants the reseller's selling rights (see 12) |
| 1.2 | Listing columns: Name, Email, Type/Permissions, KYC, Account Status, Country, Assigned Customers, Created, Actions | Columns Partner, Type, Status, Country, Currency, Guest Login, Last login, Joined. Email is a sub-line, KYC a badge inside Status, the customer count is returned but not shown | **Columns to change.** Row menu lacks Approve/Reject KYC, Manage Permissions, View Customers, View Wallet, View Commission & Payouts |
| 1.3 | Login as Guest, reason recorded, banner "Guest Session — Viewing Partner: …", Exit Guest Session, actions audited | Works. The reason is generated by the page (`autoReason`), never asked. Banner reads "Guest Mode – …", button "Exit Guest Mode". Start and end are audited, the requests in between are not | **Ask for the reason; audit each request; banner and button wording** |
| 1.3 | Partner wallet from the partner list | No wallet link on any partner screen | **Add** |
| 2.1 | Invitation, acceptance, verification, password set-up, login/logout, forgot/reset, sessions | Exists (`/partner/accept-invitation`, shared auth) | None |
| 2.2 | Before KYC approval and Active: only onboarding, business profile, KYC, security, support | A draft gets **no** permission at all, so it cannot raise a support ticket; the workspace redirects it to the KYC page. A partner active **before KYC existed** ("legacy") keeps every commercial right. Nothing checks KYC except activation | **Onboarding tier for drafts (dashboard, tickets, profile read).** **Explicit, Admin-chosen policy for legacy partners** (see ADR-063 §3) |
| 2.3 | KYC review | Exists (review, correction, approve, reject with reason, reviewer, date, access log) | None |
| 3 | Exactly eight sidebar menus | Fourteen entries per workspace, some drawn locked with an upgrade prompt; statuses are filters already | **Rebuild the menu as eight entries with tabs; show only what the partner may use** |
| 4.1.1 | KYC / account status card on the dashboard | Missing (KYC lives only on the KYC page) | **Add** |
| 4.1.2 | Clicks, registrations, eligible conversions, renewals | Clicks and conversions for an affiliate only; renewals missing | **Add renewals; show for every partner that promotes** |
| 4.1.3 | Platform Wallet balance apart from earnings | Earnings card exists; Platform Wallet not shown to partners | **Add** |
| 4.2 | Quick actions: copy referral link, activate customer plan, add money, contact support | Partly; Add Money missing; nothing about onboarding before approval | **Rebuild** |
| 5.1 | Eligible products with plans, purchase rates, commission terms; generate link; copy Store referral link | Split over Store, Store Console and Pricing pages; no Store-wide referral link | **One Eligible Products page; Store referral link** |
| 5.2 | Referral results | Exists (links, leads, conversions) | Renewal references (with 4.1.2) |
| 5.3 | Sales resources: creatives, videos, guides, documents | Product images and the approved description only | **Add videos (product media) and sales guides (help content for partners)** |
| 6.1 | Assigned organizations with actions | Exists inside one page | View customer, assigned plans |
| 6.2 | Products & setup: plans, validity, members, setup requirements and review, product and app links | Setup records exist (`provisioning.CustomerSetup`) but partners cannot see them | **Add** |
| 6.3 | Plan activation paid from the Platform Wallet at the approved partner price, atomic, idempotent, exact amount shown | **Missing.** A partner's order bills the customer, or (for `partner_billed` accounts) is fulfilled with nothing charged. No code ever debits a partner wallet | **Build** (ADR-063 §5) |
| 7.1 | Subscriptions with filters Active / Pending Setup / Expiring / Expired; renew, change plan, history | Plain table, no filters, no renew | **Build** |
| 7.2 | Partner invoices, wallet payments, payment and refund status, customer invoice references | Customer orders only; partner payments (upgrade fees) not listed | **Build "Billing records"** |
| 8 | Platform Wallet: balance, Add Money, top-up status, history, transactions | Staff-only, offline top-ups only (confirmed in `wallets/admin.py`); tests forbid a partner wallet route | **Build: partner read, online top-up through the existing partner checkout, debits for activation, renewal and plan changes** |
| 9.1 | Pending / available / reversed earnings, source, references, clearance, renewal eligibility | Exists (commission ledger, balance) | Kept |
| 9.2 | Verified bank/UPI, request, statuses, history, cap at available | Exists (withdrawals cap at available, staff verification) | Kept |
| 9 | Suspended partner commission per Super Admin policy | Accrual continues while suspended; payouts are held; no setting | **Admin policy setting** |
| 10.1 | Tickets | Exists | Kept |
| 10.2 | Partner FAQs, guides, setup instructions | Help content exists for the Admin with a `partner` audience, but partners cannot read it | **Add a Help Content page** |
| 11.1 | Business profile, KYC, history | Profile in Account, KYC on its own page outside the workspace | **One Business & KYC tab** |
| 11.2 | Staff and security, sign out other sessions | Exists except "sign out other sessions" | **Add** |
| 11.3 | White Label settings only with approved White Label | Gated by the branding/domain permissions | Domain instructions, app links, request changes |
| 12 | White Label must not grant reseller or affiliate rights | The White Label bundle includes the reseller's selling, commission and payout grants | **White Label becomes branding and domain only; base permissions are chosen explicitly** |
| 12 | Every protected action checks identity, KYC, active, permission, role, ownership | Identity, status, permission, role, ownership checked; KYC only at activation | Covered by the onboarding tier and the legacy policy |
| 13 | Shared records | Partners already read the shared orders, subscriptions, ledger, commissions and tickets | Wallet-paid sales use the same Order, Subscription and WalletEntry rows |
| 14 | Security | CSRF, rate limits, audit, safe uploads, scoped guest sessions exist | Guest per-request audit (1.3) |
| 15 | Tests | Suites exist for most areas | New backend tests, vitest and a real-browser check |
