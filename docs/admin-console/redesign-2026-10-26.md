# Super Admin console redesign (2026-10-26)

The owner's reference image: a deep-navy top bar and sidebar, a dashboard of figure tiles, three charts and three "recent" tables. This page records what was built, what deliberately differs from the image, and where it lives.

## What changed

- **Shell** (`components/sidebar/SidebarShell.tsx`, `variant="admin"`): navy top bar and sidebar (new `--w360-shell*` tokens in `app/globals.css`, contrast-checked in `theme-tokens.test.ts`), an "Admin panel" heading, the light wordmark, the name beside the avatar. The partner portal and the organization console are unchanged.
- **Menu** (`features/controlplane/adminNav.ts`): Dashboard, then collapsible sections - User (Users, Subscriptions, Entitlements), Partner (Partner dashboard, All Partners, Add Partner), Product Store (Product manager, Plans & add-ons, Currency & pricing, Orders, Payments), Communication (Email & templates, AI), Staff (Staff access, Audit, Diagnostics), Settings (Platform settings). Every page is still shown only to a holder of its grant; a section with no page left is hidden; the section of the current page opens by itself.
- **Top bar** (`features/controlplane/AdminTopbar.tsx`): *Search anything* (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K) finds console pages by name and searches users (`/admin/users?q=`) or partners (`/admin/partners/all?q=`); *New* lists only what the staff member may create (Add partner, Add product, New plan, Grant staff access); the bell reads `GET /api/v1/admin/attention`; the settings shortcut.
- **Dashboard** (`features/controlplane/AdminDashboard.tsx`, charts in `dashboardCharts.tsx`): a month picker (this month and the eleven before), tiles for Total Users, Total Partners, Total Products, Paid Orders and Active Subscriptions, a Revenue Overview line, a User Registration column chart, a Product-wise Sales donut, and Recent Users / Orders / Partners (five rows each, "View all" to the list).
- **Backend**: `GET /api/v1/admin/dashboard?month=YYYY-MM` gains an `overview` section (`commercial/controlplane/dashboard.py`), each part behind the grant of the screen it summarises; `GET /api/v1/admin/attention` lists what waits for staff (upgrade requests, draft partners, payouts). New counting selectors live in the module that owns each table (identity, partners, catalog, payments). No migration.

## Where it differs from the image, on purpose

| In the image | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Total Messages | Paid Orders | Messages are tenant rows behind row-level security; there is no platform-wide message count to show honestly. |
| Open Tickets, HelpDesk menu, Recent Tickets | Active Subscriptions, no HelpDesk entry, Recent Partners | Help Desk is a separate product with its own data; the platform holds no tickets. |
| One revenue figure | Per currency (a picker when there are several) | Amounts are never converted or added across currencies. |
| White "New" text on green | Navy text on green | White on the brand green is 2.4:1 (fails WCAG). |
| % beside every figure | % only where there is a base | Tiles show growth of the total (added / what was there before); charts compare with the period before - the same days of the previous month while a month is running. No % is shown when the base is zero. |

Days are UTC days (the platform's time zone).
