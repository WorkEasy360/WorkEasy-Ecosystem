# ADR-064: The User Admin Panel: seven menus, the customer's three wallets, and Login as Guest beside View

Date: 2026-11-11. Status: accepted (built on `feat/partner-admin-panel`, not committed, not deployed). Amends ADR-059 (each portal's own sign-in page), ADR-060 (wallets readable and fundable by staff only), ADR-062 and ADR-063 (where Login as Guest sits and what its record holds), and the 2026-11-03 consolidation of the organization console's sidebar into 13 entries.

## Context

The owner's brief "WorkEasy360 – User Admin Panel Update" (specification of 08 November 2026), with two reference images, asked for the customer console at `app.workeasy360.com` to be redesigned and completed, and for one correction in the Super Admin. The audit found:

- **The console listed 13 entries** under six headings (Product Hub, Billing & Orders, Profile, Product Access, Integrations & Automation, AI, Analytics, Login & Authentication, Security Center, Activity & Audit, Support...) in its own light shell, unlike the Admin and Partner consoles.
- **The customer could not see its own wallets.** The three ledgers (Platform Wallet, AI Credits, Consumption Wallet; ADR-060) existed per organization, but only staff could read them or place a top-up.
- **No feed for the top bar**, and the unused `NotificationPreference` table.
- **The billing account held no business name or address.**
- **The subscriptions read did not say who collects a subscription**, so the console could not tell a partner-paid subscription from one the customer manages.
- **Login as Guest was the last column of the partner tables** (after "Created"), with the row's other actions past it, and only in the partner page's "More actions" menu. Its banner had one exit, and its start was audited without the partner's type.
- The customer sign-in was the plain card the product sign-in uses, not the reference image.

## Decision

1. **Exactly seven menus, for everyone** (`features/launcher/orgNav.ts`): Dashboard, My Products, Billing & Subscriptions, Wallets & Credits, Organization & Team, Help, Settings. A menu with more than one entry is a disclosure whose sub-menu opens with it (one open at a time); an entry with several pages draws them as tabs above the page. Every earlier address is still served under its menu (tested page by page). The seven are drawn **whatever the member's permissions**: a menu none of whose pages the member may open leads to its first page, which says who can help ("Contact Organization Admin"); entries inside a menu still appear only with their permission. Navigation is never security - the backend authorizes every request.
   - The organization's integrations, automations, AI settings, analytics and the delivery log stay where they were, as **Settings → Integrations & AI**, rather than being removed: they are platform-level features already in use, not product operations. Product-specific builders remain inside the products.
2. **The consoles' one shell.** The org console uses the Admin/Partner variant of `SidebarShell` (teal frame, green current page, light sheet), the accordion, the icon rail (`w360.org.rail`) and the drawer below 1024px, and sets `<html data-console="user">` so the shared console rules (type scale, 36px controls, card radius) and the kit apply; tab titles end "· WorkEasy360". The top bar holds the **organization switcher** (a menu only when the person belongs to more than one organization), a **search** over the member's own pages and the organization's products (Ctrl/⌘K), the **bell**, Help, and the account menu (My Profile, Account & Security, Logout).
3. **The dashboard reads only real state** (`features/console/UserDashboard.tsx`), each section through the member's own permission and left out - never shown as zero - when the member may not see it: products from the app directory, setup records, trial states; team from the console dashboard summary; subscriptions; **spend = paid orders in the period in the billing currency** (this month, last month, the last 90 days; the change against the period before only when there was spend then); the three wallets on separate lines; pending actions from the bell's feed; recent activity from the audit log (administrators) or the person's own account activity. **No invented figures**: the reference image's "+20% vs last month" exists only for spend, the one figure with history.
4. **The customer's wallets** (`commercial/wallets/customer.py`, `interfaces/api/v1/org_account_views.py`): `GET wallets`, `wallets/transactions`, `wallets/usage` behind `platform.billing.view`; `POST wallets/top-up` and `wallets/credit-packs` behind `platform.billing.manage` (`payments.wallet_orders.member_top_up` / `member_credit_pack`). The organization always comes from the verified tenant context. **A top-up is an order**, exactly as staff's (ADR-060): the member pays its invoice through the existing hosted checkout, and only the paid order's fulfilment credits the wallet, once - a failed or abandoned payment moves nothing. Orders are placed in the billing account's currency only. The three balances are never added together anywhere. No new permission keys: the owner and **Billing Managers** hold billing rights; the Administrator role deliberately does not (it is "separable from admin on purpose", `authz.SYSTEM_ROLES`), so an administrator sees "Contact Organization Admin" on spending actions.
5. **The bell** (`commercial/controlplane/customer_alerts.py`): `GET notifications` derives product setup waiting for the customer, renewals within 14 days, payments awaiting the customer (an open invoice is "overdue" only once its due day has passed - on immediate terms it is "due today" on the day it is issued), low balances (only where a threshold is set) and support replies awaiting the customer - each only for a member who may see that data - and respects the member's **in-app preferences** (`GET/PUT notification-preferences`, the existing `NotificationPreference` table on the `in_app` channel; absence means on). Read state is kept on the browser.
6. **Billing Profile**: `billing.0003_billing_profile` adds business name and address fields to `BillingAccount` (empty defaults; overlap-safe). `PATCH billing-account` behind `platform.billing.manage` changes only the profile fields; currency, collection mode and region stay Workeasy360's.
7. **Purchase source**: the subscriptions read gains `collection_mode`. A partner-paid subscription (`partner_wallet`) is managed through the partner (no web cancel); an app-store source would show the store's own management link - the console handles it, the backend does not emit it yet.
8. **The customer portal's sign-in** is the User Admin Login of the reference image (`features/auth/UserLogin.tsx`), on the Admin/Partner sign-in frame; its sign-up and password pages wear the same frame. A product sign-in (`?resume=`), a partner's branded sign-in (`?brand=`) and the central sign-in host keep the plain page.
9. **Login as Guest beside View** (Super Admin → Partners, all four lists): a "View / Actions" column, fourth after Name, Type and Status, holds **View** and then **Login as Guest**, with the row's other actions in the ⋯ beside them; the old trailing column is gone and Earnings is not reintroduced. The partner's page has Back to Partners, Edit Partner and Login as Guest in its header. The banner reads "Guest Session – Viewing Partner: <name>", names the partner type in the Admin's words (the list it is filed under: Affiliate, Reseller, White Label), and offers **Return to Super Admin** (back to the list or page it started from, its search and filters restored from the tab) and **End Guest Session** (to the partner's page in the Admin); both end the session on the server first. The guest view's start audit now records the partner's **category** at that moment.

## Consequences

- The customer can now spend from its console; the money path is the one staff already used, so no new settlement or refund logic exists.
- An Administrator who needs to pay must be given the Billing Manager role. Said in the UI.
- The free AI allowance is counted in AI tokens (the rate's unit), and purchased credits do not expire in the model: the page shows the monthly reset date of the allowance only.
- There is still no invoice PDF endpoint (the invoice prints from the browser), no customer refund endpoint (Request refund opens a billing ticket), and customers cannot close a ticket on the Workeasy360 desk.
- The server keeps a guest view's return path without its query string; the list's filters come back from the browser tab instead.
