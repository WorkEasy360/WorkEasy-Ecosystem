# ADR-048: Simplified Super Admin partner management - Dashboard, All Partners, Add Partner, and Complete Your Profile

Status: accepted, 2026-10-23. Builds on ADR-041 (Partner Control Center), ADR-042 (setup, profile and contacts), ADR-047 (admin-created partners and type workspaces). Changes ADR-047 decision 3 (what Add Partner creates) and ADR-041's local navigation; keeps every other decision.

## Context

The Super Admin Partners area had grown nine entries (Dashboard, All Partners, Resellers, White-label, Affiliates, Customers, Products & Pricing, Commissions, Payouts) and a seven-step Add Partner form that asked for business details, products, prices and commission before the account existed. The owner asked for three entries - **Dashboard, All Partners, Add Partner** - a short creation form (name, administrator email and password, type, optional country), and all detailed setup done later, per partner, in a **Complete Your Profile** wizard whose steps depend on what the partner is. Backend functionality is kept; the architecture of ADR-047 (one `PartnerAccount`, capabilities decide everything, type = preset) is unchanged.

## Decisions

1. **The Partners area has three entries** (`partnerCenterNav.ts`): Dashboard (`/admin/partners`), All Partners (`/admin/partners/all`, which also owns a partner's own pages) and Add Partner (`/admin/partners/new`, staff with `staff_partner.manage`). Resellers, White-label and Affiliates are the **Partner type filter** of All Partners; customers, products, prices, commission, payouts, branding and domains are sections of **one partner's page**.
2. **Old addresses redirect** (`oldPartnerRoutes.ts`): the three type lists to All Partners filtered on the type (keeping a search and a valid status); customers, products, commissions and payouts (and `/admin/commissions`, `/admin/payouts`) to All Partners; `resellers/new` and `white-label/new` to Add Partner with the type preselected; `white-label/<id>` to that partner's Complete Your Profile at the brand step. The cross-partner screens behind them (all-partner customers, commission ledger and payout queue, white-label list, Add Reseller wizard, white-label setup wizard) were removed from the frontend; their backend reads and commands are unchanged and still used by the per-partner sections.
3. **Add Partner creates a draft account and nothing else** (`partner_onboarding.create`). Request: `{experience, partner: {display_name, country?}, administrator: {email, mode, password, password_confirm}}`; any other key is refused (so a browser cannot add capabilities, products, prices or a status). One transaction creates or links the user (ADR-047 decisions 4-5 unchanged: hash only, never overwrite an existing account, no temporary password, no email), the `PartnerAccount` in **draft**, the country on the profile, the type's capability bundle (`grant_experience`, `make_default=True`) and the owner membership. The partner code is derived from the name under an advisory lock (`acme`, `acme-2`, ...); the preferred currency starts from the country (platform default otherwise). `create-review` is removed (nothing left to review).
4. **The preferred (payout) currency may change only while nothing is kept in it** - the partner is a draft and has no price book of its own (`profile._check_currency_change`). After activation it is fixed, as before.
5. **Profile completion is computed on the server, in one rule with two forms** (`controlplane/profile_completion.py`). `evaluate(Facts)` lists the sections a partner needs, each `complete | incomplete | pending | optional`, the percentage of *required* sections (optional ones are shown, never counted) and what is missing; `complete_q(now)` is the same rule as a database filter, composed from partner-owned and commission-owned `Exists` expressions (F-6 layering kept). A test holds the two to the same answers over a matrix of partners. Requirements follow the **capabilities held**, never a stored type, so a combined partner needs the union:
   - every partner: business information (primary contact name and email, business email, country), a partner administrator, at least one capability;
   - Reseller or White-label capabilities: at least one product grant;
   - Reseller or Affiliate capabilities: a commission configuration (holds *Earn commission* and a rule is in force - its own or the programme default);
   - White-label capabilities: a named brand;
   - Affiliate capabilities: an active referral code;
   - optional, never counted: partner prices (list price applies otherwise) and a custom domain.
6. **"Ready" is a display state.** A draft with an incomplete profile is shown as *Setup required*, a draft with a complete one as *Ready*; the stored status is unchanged. The partner summary's `activation` is now the completion's missing list, and the console offers **Activate** only when it is empty. Activation remains a separate, reasoned staff act (the service still refuses a partner with no administrator); completion grants nothing.
7. **Complete Your Profile** (`/admin/partners/<id>/profile`, `features/controlplane/profile/`) is a step wizard whose steps follow the server's labels: Reseller - Business information, Products, Pricing, Commission, Customer & sales configuration, Sub-reseller settings, Team, Review; White-label - Business information, Brand identity, Products, Pricing, Customer configuration, Custom domain, Communication, Customer portal (optional), Team, Review; Affiliate - Profile, Products promoted, Commission, Referral program, Payout settings, Marketing information, Review; a combined partner gets the union in one order. **Each step is a panel that already existed** (business and contacts editors, product grants, partner prices, the commission rule command, capability grant/revoke as switches, the member list, the white-label brand/domain/communication steps over `useWhiteLabelContext`) - the wizard adds no new write except the staff referral code below. The completion is shown beside the steps and refreshed after every change.
8. **Staff may mint a referral code for a partner that holds `referral.create`** (`referrals.create_referral_code_for_partner`, `POST admin/partners/<id>/referral-codes`, `staff_partner.manage`): the same mint, limit and audit action as the partner's own command, refused for a terminated partner or one without the capability; a code captures nothing until the partner is active. The staff referrals read now carries each code's link, the platform's attribution window and whether a code can be made.
9. **All Partners** is one table (Partner Name, Admin Email, Partner Type badges, Profile Completion, Products, Customers, Status, Created, Last Login, Actions) with **server-side** filters: Partner type (a label = a filter over grants, so a combined partner is in every view it belongs to), Status, Profile (complete/incomplete, the SQL form of the rule) and a search over name, code, legal name, contact email, business email and any active member's sign-in email. Row actions: View Details, and Suspend / Reactivate with a recorded reason.
10. **The dashboard** counts total, active, resellers, white-label, affiliates (all over non-terminated partners, by grants), suspended and incomplete profiles, and lists the five newest partners with their completion. No revenue, MRR, ARR or growth figure.

## Alternatives

- **Keep the seven-step creation form and add the wizard beside it.** Rejected: two parallel ways to set a partner up, with two validation paths.
- **A stored "profile complete" flag** updated by each command. Rejected: it can drift from the facts; the filter is computed from the facts in SQL instead.
- **Enforcing completion in the partner status service.** Not done: existing flows and tests activate partners through that service with other readiness rules; the console gates Activate on completion and the service keeps its one hard rule (an administrator). Listed as an owner decision.
- **Per-type completion tables** (reseller fields, white-label fields). Rejected: the profile, brand, grants and rules already hold every fact; a second copy would drift.

## Not built (stated on the screens)

Brand icon, favicon, secondary colour, privacy-policy and terms URLs (the brand renders one accent and links only support pages); per-plan commission scope, per-partner hold period and a commission "type" other than a percentage (the rule engine has none); a minimum payout threshold; a marketing library; partner SMTP. A cross-partner payout approval queue and commission ledger are no longer in the navigation - payouts and commission are reached per partner.

## Consequences

Super Admin's partner navigation is three entries; detailed setup happens per partner with the server deciding what is complete. The partner portal, workspaces, authorization and ledgers are untouched. No migration.
