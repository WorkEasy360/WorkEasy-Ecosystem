# ADR-054: Product-aware affiliate links - promotion grants, product updates, paid-only referral commission

Status: accepted, 2026-10-28. Builds on ADR-029 (commission ledger), ADR-052 (recurring commission, referral cookie) and ADR-053 (typed referral links, campaigns). Status and traceability: `docs/affiliate-products/status.md`.

Nothing here adds a second tracking system, ledger, catalog or admin application. Every change extends `commissions.referrals`, `commissions.accrual`, the catalog and the existing Admin Panel and partner workspaces.

## Decisions

1. **Promote is a permission of its own.** `partners.PartnerPromotionGrant` (partner, product, active/revoked, who and when; never deleted) says which products a partner may promote. It is separate from `PartnerProductGrant` (may *sell*) and from a customer entitlement (may *use*). Only staff holding `platform.staff_partner.manage` (Super Admin, or a staff member given that one grant) assign or revoke it, and only for a partner holding `referral.create` and a product open to the affiliate channel. The check is on the server: `referrals.create_referral_code` refuses any product link without an active grant, whatever a screen sends.
2. **Link kinds.** `ReferralCode.link_kind` is `general | product | pricing | feature | update`; `ReferralCode.update` names a `catalog.ProductUpdate` for the last two. Check constraints tie kind to product and update. The destination is **derived when the link is followed** (`referrals.destination`): `/products/<slug>[?plan=]`, `/products/<slug>?view=pricing`, `/products/<slug>/updates/<update>` - always a path on the platform's own origin, never a stored URL. An archived update falls back to its product's page and a product that left the Store to sign-up, so a link never breaks and its attribution never moves. The code (4-16 letters/digits) is the link's tracking id; with it are stored the partner, product, plan, update, campaign, kind and creation time. The active-link limit is 100 per partner (was 20) so a product can have many links.
3. **Product updates.** `catalog.ProductUpdate` (feature or release; draft -> published -> archived; never deleted) holds the approved title, summary, promotional text, an optional banner (one of the product's own approved images) and an optional `landing_url`. Staff manage them in Product Manager -> Updates (`commercial.catalog.manage`). A published update of a Store product has a public page (`public/products/<slug>/updates/<update>`). `landing_url` must be https on an approved host - `W360_AFFILIATE_LANDING_HOSTS` or the host of the product's own marketing page - and is **shown** on that page, never redirected to. The address of a published update is fixed.
4. **Attribution policy (Super Admin).** `commissions.ReferralPolicy` (one row; not named after a partner type, per the ADR-047 rule): `attribution_days` (1-365; default `W360_REFERRAL_CAPTURE_DAYS`, 90) and `revoked_links` (`stop`, the default, or `continue`). The window is stamped on each capture (`expires_at`), so a change applies to new clicks only. The referral cookie now lives exactly as long as the window (it was 30 days against a 90-day window; `REFERRAL_COOKIE_MAX_AGE` is removed).
5. **First touch, enforced.** A capture made while the browser holds a live referral token (captured, unconverted, unexpired) records the click for the clicked link's figures under a token nobody is given, and returns the token the browser already holds; the cookie is not replaced. Before this, the capture endpoint overwrote the cookie, so the last click won while every document said first touch. Competing links, a partner's other links and repeat visits of the same link all follow this one rule; unique visitors are counted by the visitor-cookie hash.
6. **Revocation.** Revoking a promotion grant refuses new links at once and refuses re-enabling a switched-off link. Under `stop` the partner's existing links for the product take no new visitors (the capture is refused with the same uniform 404); under `continue` they keep working. Referrals already captured still convert, and the ledger is never touched.
7. **Commission only from a verified paid transaction (referrals).** A referral sale accrues only once the subscription line's first invoice line is **paid** (`payments.paid_item_lines`); the ledger row records it in the new `CommissionAccrual.payment_ref` (`payments.invoice_line:<uuid>`; renewals record their own line). An unpaid referred sale is skipped as `awaiting_payment` and accrues when the payment lands (listener or sweep). Managed (reseller) sales keep their existing rule. Idempotency is unchanged: one accrual per sale (`accrual:<source_ref>`, unique), so a replayed webhook, a retried task and the sweep collapse to one row.
8. **A product link earns on its product.** A referral through a product, pricing, feature or update link earns only on sales of that product (`not_the_promoted_product` otherwise). A general link earns on any product open to affiliates, as before.
9. **Refunds reverse proportionally.** When a sale's paying invoice is refunded while its commission is in the holdback, a reversal of the commission times the refunded share of what was paid is appended (half-up, integers, never more than the commission), topped up as further refunds land, idempotent per refunded amount (`reversal:<accrual>:refund:<refunded_minor>`). After the holdback a refund stays a staff decision (`ledger` reversal), as for renewals. Chargebacks are not modelled by `payments`; staff record them with the existing `chargeback` reversal reason.
10. **Funnel and statuses.** `Referral.trial_started_at` is stamped once from the customer's first `ProductTrial` (beside accrual, after a commercial change and in the sweep). Every ledger row now carries a status - `pending` (in the holdback), `approved` (cleared, payable), `reversed` (fully reversed) or `paid` (in a paid payout) - computed from the ledger and payout lines, never stored.
11. **Screens.** Affiliate: **My Products** under Promote (assigned products, their public features, published updates, a link generator by kind, links with rename / switch off / switch on / copy, per-product, per-update and per-campaign clicks, leads, trials, conversions, revenue and commission; products no longer assigned listed apart). Conversions and the admin Referrals tab gain an attribution trail (link, steps, each paid sale with its verified payment and status; the customer is a short reference, never a name or id). Admin: Partner -> Products -> "Products this partner may promote"; Product Manager -> Updates; Partner Programme -> Affiliate attribution policy.

## Consequences

- Migrations: `catalog.0006` (one table), `partners.0011` (one table), `commissions.0006` (one table, four nullable/defaulted columns, two check constraints, and a data step that marks existing product links `product` and gives each partner with an active product link the promotion grant for that product, so no live link stops working under `stop`). The append-only ledger is not rewritten (`ADD COLUMN ... DEFAULT`).
- Rule change for referred customers sold to without payment (an invoiced or staff-entered sale): no referral commission until the invoice is paid. The Phase 7 test that asserted accrual on such a sale now pays the invoice first.
- Staff scope is role-based: there is no per-partner staff scoping on the platform. "Admins within their own scope" means staff holding `platform.staff_partner.manage`; Super Admin can give that one grant to an affiliate manager.
