# ADR-056: Full-stack Store upgrade - one catalog, one price, one commercial engine

Status: accepted, 2026-11-01. Builds on ADR-051 (Product Manager), ADR-052 (cart), ADR-053 (margins, storefront), ADR-054/055 (affiliate grants, Store Console). Status, audit and traceability: `docs/store-upgrade/status.md`.

The owner's brief asked for a professional marketplace Store across Super Admin, customer, affiliate, reseller and white-label, with **one source of truth** for catalog, pricing and commercial rules. The audit found that the architecture already had one catalog, one immutable effective-dated price table and one `resolve_price`; the gaps were specific. This ADR closes them **without a second catalog, price table, price rule or ledger.**

## Decisions

1. **Promotional discount and set-up fee live on the price row.** `PlanPrice.discount_bp` and `PlanPrice.setup_fee_minor` (migration `pricing.0004`) are commercial terms of the immutable row: the publish trigger now freezes them with the amount. Starting or ending a discount, or changing a fee, is a new row with its own window, so every past order stays reproducible. `pricing.domain.apply_discount` is the **one** discount function: `resolve_price` (what an order costs) and the list reads (`direct_list_prices`, `current_prices`) both call it, so a Store card and a checkout cannot disagree. In the customer-facing list read `amount_minor` is the price after the discount, with `list_amount_minor` beside it.
2. **Effective until, scheduled changes, cancellation.** `publish_price(valid_to=...)` ends a row at a known date. A new price cuts back every row still running when it starts (open-ended or ending later). Backdating stays refused. `cancel_scheduled_price` removes a change that has **not started** (nothing can reference it) and restores the previous row's end; a started price is only ever closed. Audit entries carry the previous terms.
3. **Set-up fee at checkout.** Charged with a plan's first paid purchase (single plan or cart), never with a trial or a renewal, as its own invoice line naming no product or plan. It stays unlinked from the subscription line, so the commission ledger (which earns only on lines naming a subscription line) never pays a share of it. Partner-billed approved sales (no payment step) do not charge it.
4. **Commission rules: plan, fixed, duration.** `CommissionRule.plan_code` (a plan rule outranks a product rule outranks the default), `fixed_amount_minor` + `fixed_currency` (paid per qualifying payment in that currency only; nothing on a plan change's increase) and `recurring_months` (a recurring rule stops earning that many months after the sale). Migration `commissions.0007` freezes the new columns and `recurring`, which ADR-052 had left out of the trigger. Selection and amounts stay pure (`domain.select_rule`, `domain.rule_amount`, `domain.within_recurring_window`). Commission still accrues only from a verified payment.
5. **Partner price ranges.** `partners.SellingPriceRange` (migration `partners.0014`): per programme (Reseller / White-label), plan, cycle and currency, `min_minor`, `max_minor`, `partner_may_set`. Staff set them (`platform.staff_pricing.manage`, audited with the previous value). A partner sets its own customer price (`partners.price_ranges.set_own_price`: `platform.partner_product.manage`, active, holding the sell capability, may sell the plan, inside the range of the programme it sells under) and the price is published into **its own price book** through `pricing.publish_own_partner_price` - the ordinary immutable price row that `resolve_price` already charges partner customers. No partner price is computed anywhere else.
6. **One commercial engine.** `commissions.channel_pricing` answers, for channel `direct | affiliate | reseller | white_label`: list price, discount, base price, customer price, set-up fee, partner cost and margin **or** commission, partner share, platform share, tax treatment and payable amount. It owns no rule: prices come from `resolve_price`/the list reads, margins from `select_margin` + `partner_cost`, commissions from `select_rule` + `rule_amount` - the functions checkout and the accrual use. `compose` is pure; `resolve_channel_price` is exact (one plan), `channel_prices` is the bulk read for screens. The partner "Pricing & Margins" read now uses it instead of its own margin arithmetic. **Tax:** no tax engine exists; the engine reports each price's tax treatment and `tax_minor = null`.
7. **One experience rule.** `partners.selectors.selling_experience_at` (White-label while the partner holds the brand capability, else Reseller) is used by the accrual's margin selection, the engine and the price ranges.
8. **Merchandising is presentation.** `Product.subcategory`, `tags`, `highlights`, `benefits`, `use_cases`, `featured`, `popular`, `badge_new` and `Product.recommended_plan` (migration `catalog.0007`). The recommended plan is a reference on the product - one per product by construction - not a boolean on the plan tables, which carry no booleans (features live in `PlanFeature`; the catalog suite pins that). Reads expose a derived `recommended` per plan. The shared Store browse engine adds curated collections (featured / popular / new) as a validated filter with counts, and searches tags and subcategory.
9. **Customer product page.** `GET orgs/<slug>/store/<product>` uses Explore's own listing (so visibility, partner narrowing and trial state are unchanged) and adds content, screenshots, a plan comparison from the plan versions in force, what the organization holds and related products.
10. **Super Admin Store management.** `controlplane.store_admin` + `/api/v1/admin/store/...`: dashboard (reads of projections only), product-wise commercial rules (every channel's engine figures per plan; edits go to `create_rule_version`, `set_margin`, `price_ranges`, the channel flags), plans & pricing board (current and scheduled prices per book), duplicate plan (a draft copy with features and current prices), engine preview, price close/cancel. Screens: Store -> Overview / Browse / Commercial rules; Product -> Plans & Pricing.

## Consequences

- Migrations `pricing.0004`, `catalog.0007`, `commissions.0007`, `partners.0014`: additive, every new NOT NULL column has a database default; reversible (the triggers are recreated with their previous column lists). `tenancy.0001` now declares its existing dependency on `sessions` (it grants on `django_session`), which the new migrations' dependencies on tenancy made necessary.
- Existing subscriptions keep the price snapshot they were bought at, discount included; a discount that ends affects only later purchases.
- Not built: a tax engine; campaign-specific commission rules (campaigns are partner-owned labels); live payment gateway credentials (owner step, unchanged).
