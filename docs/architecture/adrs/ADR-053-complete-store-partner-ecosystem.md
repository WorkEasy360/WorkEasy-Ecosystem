# ADR-053: Complete Store + partner ecosystem - margins, partner orders, branded store, affiliate links, additive workspaces, set-up fees

Status: accepted, 2026-10-28. Implements the owner's *Complete Store + Partner Ecosystem* brief on top of ADR-051 (Product Manager) and ADR-052 (cart, eligibility, recurring commission). Owner decisions taken on 2026-10-28 are marked **(owner)**. Status and traceability: `docs/ecosystem-complete/status.md`.

Nothing here adds a parallel partner type, user store, catalog, order, subscription, entitlement or commission engine. Every change extends the existing services; authorization stays with `PartnerMember`, capability and product grants, partner status and `StaffGrant`, and is re-checked on every request.

## Decisions

1. **Margin = discount off list (owner).** `commissions.MarginRule` is a versioned rule like `CommissionRule` (only `retired_at` moves; a trigger freezes the rest): either a programme default for an experience (`reseller`, `white_label`) or a per-partner override, optionally per product. **Partner cost** = list price (default direct book at the moment of sale) less `discount_bp`, half-up; **customer price** = the partner's book, else list; **margin** = customer price - cost. A *managed* sale (a partner's own customer) under a margin rule accrues the margin in the one ledger **instead of** a commission - never both (`CommissionAccrual.earning = margin`, with `margin_rule`, `cost_minor`, and the effective margin as `rate_bp`). A plan increase earns the margin rate on the increase. Margins recur on paid renewals unless the rule says otherwise. A referral (affiliate) sale always earns commission. No margin rule = commission exactly as before. Staff manage margins on Currency & Pricing (`/admin/margins`, `platform.staff_commission.manage`); partners read cost, customer price, margin and margin % per plan (`partner/<id>/plans`), all computed on the server.
2. **A reseller's sale is a central Order.** `partner/<id>/customers/<org>/orders[/quote]` places an ordinary `payments` order for the customer with the partner as **seller** (`Order.channel = partner`, `Order.partner_id`). `payments` sits below `partners`, so the partner layer passes a `Seller` (channel, partner id, `may_sell`) into `orders.quote/place`; the order sells only plans the partner's grants and switches allow and prices from the partner's book. A customer on automatic collection pays the order's invoice before anything is granted; an invoiced or partner-billed account is an *approved sale*, fulfilled at once with nothing charged now. The old `sell` endpoint stays for API compatibility. Orders list the plans, the earning (margin or commission, from the ledger) and the payment status for the partner.
3. **Branded store on a path (owner).** `app.workeasy360.com/store/<partner code>` (public to browse) shows the partner's brand over the central catalog: only its sellable products (grant + white-label channel + its own sale/Explore switches), at its prices. Buying adds `storefront=<code>` to the platform's own checkout; the server re-verifies the store (`partners.storefront.seller_for`), prices from the partner's book and refuses a customer that already belongs to another partner. When the order is **fulfilled** the buyer becomes the partner's customer (a `PartnerOrganizationLink`, made inside fulfilment's transaction through the `orders.on_storefront_fulfil` registration, before the line exists), so margin/commission accrue through the managed path and the console shows the partner's brand. A failed payment links nothing. Sign-in on partner custom domains stays unsupported (D-035); the verified domain keeps its branded landing.
4. **Affiliate links (owner brief §3).** `ReferralCode` gains `product_code`, `plan_code`, `campaign`; a partner may choose its code (4-16 letters/digits with a letter, unique). `ReferralCampaign` is a partner-owned grouping. The public link is `<app>/r/<code>` (served by the sign-in portal so the referral cookie is the one sign-up reads); the server answers a **path it derived** from the product (`/products/<slug>[?plan=]` or `/signup`) - never a stored URL, so no open redirect. `Referral` gains `visitor_hash` (SHA-256 of an HttpOnly first-party visitor id) for unique visitors and `registered_at` (stamped when the visitor verifies a new account) for Registrations. Commission still accrues only from a verified, paid sale; the ledger row now records `product_code` and `plan_code`, which feed conversions, top products and per-link/per-campaign sales and commission. Marketing materials are the Product Manager's approved listing (logo, banner, screenshots, descriptions) of products open to affiliates, served publicly for Store products (`public/products/<slug>/media/<id>`); affiliates read them and cannot change the catalog.
5. **Additive workspaces.** `capabilities.SUBSUMED_BY` is now empty: a partner holding the White-label bundle (which includes the reseller commercial set) is offered the Reseller workspace beside the White-label one; an affiliate upgraded to both switches between Affiliate, Reseller and White-label (the switcher lists them in that order). The workspace and landing preference stay presentation only.
6. **Set-up fees, settled offline (owner).** `partners.PartnerProgramFee` holds an optional fee per target (`reseller`, `white_label`), set by staff (`/admin/partner-programme/fees`, `platform.staff_partner.manage`). A request snapshots the fee when it opens (`fee_amount_minor`, `fee_currency`); approval of a request with a fee is refused without the payment reference (stored as the commercial reference, `fee_settled_at` stamped). No online charge: the platform invoices offline.
7. **Brand fields.** The portal gains `terms_url` and `privacy_url` (plain https, displayed never fetched), shown on the branded store. A separate favicon upload is not built; branded pages use the brand logo.
8. **Support sends.** `POST partner/<id>/support` emails the request to the platform's partner-support address through the platform's notification pipeline (template `platform.partner.support_request`, reply-to the member, rate-limited, audited without the words). Easy Help Desk remains linked for tracking.

## Consequences

- Migrations (additive; new NOT NULL columns carry database defaults; the append-only ledger is not rewritten): `commissions.0005`, `payments.0004`, `partners.0010`.
- The `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` entries `partner_cost`, `affiliate_signups` and `marketing_materials` are removed: the capabilities exist.
- The live payment gateway still needs the owner's provider credentials (Stripe/Razorpay adapters exist); until then production checkout of a paid order cannot complete.
