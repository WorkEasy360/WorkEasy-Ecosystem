# ADR-052: Store cart, channel eligibility, recurring commission, referral cookie

Status: accepted, 2026-10-28. Closes the gaps the owner's *Workeasy360 Store Working Flow* PDF (10 pages) showed against the code (docs/store-flow/README.md). Builds on ADR-051 (the Product Manager) and the Phase 9 pay-first orders.

## Decisions

1. **Multi-product cart, one order, one payment** (PDF pp. 3, 9, 10). An order may carry `cart_items` (2-10 plans, one per product). `Order.kind = cart`, `Order.lines` holds each line priced by the server (its own price-book resolution and snapshot, prorated on its own when it joins a running subscription). The invoice has one line per product. Fulfilment adds every line in **one transaction** through the existing `subscriptions.system.add_paid_line`: each product becomes its own subscription line and its own entitlement; if any line can no longer be added, none is, the order fails and the payment is refunded automatically. The one-subscription-per-organization model is unchanged - the PDF's "Subscription 1/2/3" are three subscription *lines*, each with its own entitlement and its own commission. A cart starts no trial and cannot contain a free-trial plan (a trial starts one product at a time). Everything else is the existing order path: idempotency key, at most one order awaiting payment, signed webhooks, expiry, re-driven fulfilment. Migration `payments.0003` (one JSON column; the check constraint admits `cart`).
2. **The Store's address** (PDF p. 2). `/store` on the customer portal opens the organization's Explore (one organization) or the chooser (several). The cart lives in the tab (`sessionStorage`, per organization) and holds identifiers and labels only - never a price.
3. **Channel eligibility is enforced** (PDF p. 8). The Product Manager's flags now gate: a partner product grant (a branded partner - branding or domain capability - needs the product open to white-label; any other seller to reseller), every partner sale (`may_sell` re-checks, so closing a channel stops new sales at once), and affiliate commission (a referral earns nothing on a product closed to affiliates). Grants already made stay; they simply cannot sell while the channel is closed.
4. **Recurring commission** (PDF p. 6). `CommissionRule.recurring` (default **off**; migration `commissions.0004`). Under a recurring rule each **paid** renewal invoice line of an attributed customer accrues its own commission (`accrual:payments.invoice_line:<id>` - idempotent), as of the payment, with the same holdback; the first paid line of a subscription line is the sale and earns through the existing path. A referral attributes renewals for as long as the customer stays; a managed partner relationship outranks it. A renewal refunded inside the holdback is reversed automatically (`reason_code = refund`). Reconciliation evaluates renewals too. A partner's new rate keeps the rule's recurring choice. Commission still follows only verified payments, never a browser event.
5. **Referral cookie** (PDF p. 6, "attribution ID/cookie recorded"). Following a referral link also sets an HttpOnly, host-only (`__Host-` in production), SameSite=Lax cookie with the same opaque token (the server stores only its hash), for 30 days - the age limit for a referred organization. Conversion takes the page's token or, failing that, the cookie, and clears it. This fixes a real loss: the verification email opens a new tab, where the page's `sessionStorage` copy was gone.

## Not done here (owner decisions or credentials)

- **Live payment gateway**: Stripe and Razorpay adapters exist with signed-webhook verification; switching them on needs the owner's provider accounts and secrets (environment, never the repository).
- **Partner setup fees** and a **branded public storefront** on a partner's verified domain: product and pricing decisions not yet made (amounts, who collects, what the branded store lists).
- **Partner-set pricing** within admin rules: staff set partner price books today.
- A console control for the recurring switch: rules are created through the rule API (`POST admin/commission/rules`, `"recurring": true`); the console has no rule screen yet.
